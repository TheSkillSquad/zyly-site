Where the money actually goes

Three leaks, and none of them are about how the website looks

01

Enquiries die on a Friday

They land in somebody's personal WhatsApp at 17:40 and get noticed on Monday. By then the customer has phoned the next shop.

02

Quotes go out and vanish

Typed one at a time, sent, and then nobody chases them. Half of the work you nearly won is sitting in a sent folder.

03

The site loses the sale

Slow on a phone, no clear next step, and a contact form nobody has tested since it was built.

This shows up in any business that depends on enquiries and bookings — right now I'm focused on high-value, appointment-driven businesses, especially med spas and aesthetic clinics.
